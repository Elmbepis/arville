About Us

  

   

Online for several years now,  www.siliconfareast.com™ (formerly known as semiconfareast.com) is one of the world's most comprehensive references on semiconductor manufacturing that gives free and complete access to everyone.

  

Based in the Philippines, we chose the domain name www.siliconfareast.com™ because it most aptly describes what our website does - providing information about semiconductor manufacturing in Southeast Asia, where the booming semiconductor industry has deeply rooted itself over the years. 

  

Thanks to our readers' support and continuous patronage, the domain name www.siliconfareast.com™ has evolved from just being a self-descriptive internet address to a domain name that represents authoritative knowledge on semiconductor manufacturing and engineering that's completely accessible to everyone.

  

We promise to continually develop this website into the ultimate encyclopedia of semiconductor manufacturing.  This means that this website will never be finished - it will live on and on, changing week in and week out, as long as there is new knowledge to keep in its cybershelves.

  

There are always new things to learn about semiconductor manufacturing, so we welcome contributions, feedbacks, and suggestions that can help us further improve this website.
