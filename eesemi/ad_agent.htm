How to Join

     

Joining as an ad affiliate of siliconfareast.com is easy as A-B-C!  Find an advertiser first that is not yet an advertiser of ours, based on our list of advertisers. Once he or she agrees to place an ad, just email us at contact@siliconfareast.com.  If you're a first-timer, we will send you a separate email requesting you to give us additional information about yourself.

    

Don't forget to cc: your advertiser in the email.  Here's what you need to put in your email:  1) your name; 2) the advertiser's name; 3) the advertiser's contact details; 4) the ad service(s) that the advertiser will buy; 5) if applicable, which article pages they want their hyperlinked logos to appear in; and 6) what text hyperlink they want to appear under each hyperlinked logo. 

     

We will acknowledge your email and then post all the ads on our website as requested.  Once the ads have been posted, we will ask your advertiser to approve them and then pay for the ads either by direct deposit to our bank account, by check, or by credit card. Checks may be sent to our office address by registered mail or courier.

    

Once we get the payment (checks need to be cleared first), you can collect your commission in cash (US$ or PHP) from us immediately.
