SOIC/SOJ Packing in Tubes and Tape and Reel                               

                

The SOIC and the SOJ are small-outline surface-mounted plastic IC packages.  They have the same package outlines but differ in the types of leads they use.  The SOIC has gull wing leads while the SOJ has J-formed leads. 

  

SOIC and SOJ units may be shipped in tubes or tape and reel.  Table 1 below shows some packing data when these packages are shipped in tube or tape and reel.
