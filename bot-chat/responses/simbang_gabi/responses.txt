Simbang Gabi is a special Filipino tradition! It's nine days of early morning masses before Christmas, starting December 16.
Simbang Gabi means going to church very early in the morning for nine days before Christmas. After mass, families eat yummy food like bibingka and puto bumbong!
The Simbang Gabi tradition is over 400 years old! It's a beautiful way Filipino families get ready for Christmas.
