On Christmas, Filipino families enjoy lechon, ham, queso de bola, pancit, lumpia, and lots of rice! For dessert: bibingka, puto bumbong, and fruit salad.
Christmas food is the best food! In the Philippines we have ham, lechon, spaghetti, and bibingka. Yum!
Different countries have different Christmas foods. In the Philippines, Noche Buena is a big midnight feast with the whole family!
