# The Reproductive System
How living things make new living things.

## The big idea
All living things — plants, animals, and humans — can make new living things like themselves. This is called **reproduction**. Without it, life on Earth would stop.

## Two ways to reproduce

### Asexual reproduction
Only **one parent** is needed. The young is a copy of the parent.
- Bacteria split in two
- Hydras grow buds that break off
- Strawberry plants send out runners that grow new plants

### Sexual reproduction
**Two parents** are needed — a male and a female. The young has traits from both parents.
- Most animals, including humans
- Flowering plants

## Reproduction in humans (basic)
In humans, reproduction involves special cells called **sex cells** or **gametes**:
- **Sperm** — made by the male body
- **Egg** — made by the female body

When a sperm and egg join, a new life begins. This is called **fertilization**.

### Male reproductive system
- **Testes** — make sperm
- **Penis** — delivers sperm
- These organs are outside the body because sperm need to be cooler than body temperature.

### Female reproductive system
- **Ovaries** — store and release eggs
- **Fallopian tubes** — where fertilization usually happens
- **Uterus (womb)** — where a baby grows
- **Vagina** — the birth canal

## How a baby grows
1. **Fertilization** — sperm and egg join
2. **Zygote** — the first single cell of the new person
3. **Embryo** — after many cell divisions
4. **Fetus** — after about 8 weeks, when body parts form
5. **Birth** — after about 9 months (40 weeks), the baby is born

## Growth and development
After birth, humans go through stages:
- **Baby** (0–2 years) — grows fast, learns to walk and talk
- **Child** (2–12 years) — grows steadily, learns at school
- **Teenager** (13–19 years) — body changes, becomes able to reproduce
- **Adult** (20+ years) — fully grown, may have children of their own

## Reproduction in other living things
- **Flowering plants** — pollen fertilizes eggs inside flowers, seeds form
- **Frogs** — eggs laid in water, sperm fertilizes them outside the body
- **Birds** — lay eggs that hatch outside the mother's body
- **Mammals** — babies grow inside the mother and are born live

## Why this matters
Understanding reproduction helps us:
- Understand how families grow
- Take care of our bodies
- Care for babies and children
- Respect that all living things have life cycles

## Try this at home
Look at a flowering plant. Can you find the stamen (male part) and pistil (female part)? Those are the plant's reproductive organs. When pollen moves from one flower to another, seeds can form.
