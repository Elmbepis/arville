# Weather
What the sky is doing right now — and what it might do next.

## What is weather?
Weather is what the air around us is doing at a particular time and place. Is it sunny? Cloudy? Rainy? Windy? Hot? Cold? All of these describe the weather.

Weather changes from day to day, and sometimes from hour to hour. The weather in your town might be different from the weather in a town just a few kilometers away.

## What makes up weather?
Weather has several parts. Scientists measure each one:

- **Temperature** — how hot or cold the air is
- **Wind** — moving air. Wind has speed (how fast) and direction (which way it blows)
- **Humidity** — how much water vapor is in the air
- **Rain** — water falling from clouds
- **Clouds** — collections of tiny water droplets or ice crystals floating in the sky

## Weather instruments
Scientists use special tools to measure weather:

- **Thermometer** — measures temperature
- **Anemometer** — measures wind speed
- **Rain gauge** — measures how much rain fell
- **Barometer** — measures air pressure
- **Weather vane** — shows wind direction

## Weather in the Philippines
The Philippines has two main seasons: the **wet season** and the **dry season**.

- **Wet season** — usually June to November. Heavy rain and typhoons.
- **Dry season** — usually December to May. Little rain, sunny and hot.

## Weather disturbances
The Philippines experiences many **weather disturbances** — sudden changes that bring heavy rain, strong winds, or both. The most important are **typhoons**.

## Why weather matters
Weather affects what we wear, what we eat, how we travel, and even how we feel. Farmers depend on rain to grow crops. Fishermen watch the wind and waves before going out to sea. Pilots check the weather before flying. Knowing the weather helps us stay safe and plan our day.

## Try this at home
Keep a simple weather chart for one week. Every morning, write down: Is it sunny, cloudy, or rainy? Is it hot, warm, or cool? Is it windy? At the end of the week, look for patterns.
