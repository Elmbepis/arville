# The Circulatory System
How your heart, blood, and blood vessels work together.

## Your body's delivery system
Every part of your body needs **oxygen** and **nutrients** to work. Your body also needs to remove waste like **carbon dioxide**. The **circulatory system** is the delivery system that carries all of this around. It has three main parts: the **heart**, the **blood**, and the **blood vessels**.

## The heart
Your heart is a strong muscle about the size of your fist. It's located in the middle of your chest, slightly to the left. The heart's job is to **pump blood** all around your body.

A heart beats about 60 to 100 times per minute when you're resting. When you run or play, it beats faster to deliver more oxygen to your muscles. Over a lifetime, an average heart beats more than 2 billion times.

The heart has four chambers — two on the left, two on the right. Blood flows through them in a specific order, guided by valves that keep it moving in one direction.

## Blood
Blood is a liquid made of several parts:
- **Red blood cells** — carry oxygen. They contain a protein called **hemoglobin** that grabs oxygen and holds it.
- **White blood cells** — fight germs and infections.
- **Platelets** — help blood clot when you get a cut.
- **Plasma** — the yellowish liquid that carries everything else.

Blood is red because of the hemoglobin in red blood cells. When it's carrying oxygen, it's bright red. When it's not, it's darker red.

## Blood vessels
Blood travels through three types of vessels:
- **Arteries** — carry blood **away** from the heart. They have thick, strong walls because the blood is under high pressure.
- **Veins** — carry blood **back** to the heart. They have thinner walls.
- **Capillaries** — tiny, thin vessels that connect arteries to veins. They're so narrow that blood cells pass through in single file.

## The journey of blood
1. The heart pumps oxygen-rich blood out through the **arteries**.
2. The blood travels to **capillaries** in your body's tissues.
3. Oxygen and nutrients pass from the blood into the cells.
4. Carbon dioxide and waste pass from the cells into the blood.
5. The blood flows into **veins** and returns to the heart.
6. The heart pumps it to the lungs, where it picks up fresh oxygen.
7. The cycle repeats.

## Keeping it healthy
- **Exercise** strengthens your heart.
- **Eating fruits and vegetables** keeps your blood vessels healthy.
- **Drinking water** keeps your blood flowing well.
- **Sleeping enough** helps your whole body, including your heart.

## Try this at home
Find your **pulse** by pressing two fingers on the inside of your wrist, just below your thumb. Count the beats for 15 seconds, then multiply by 4. That's your heart rate per minute. Now do 20 jumping jacks and check again. Did it change? Why?
