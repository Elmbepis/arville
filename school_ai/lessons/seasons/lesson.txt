# Seasons
Why the weather changes through the year.

## What are seasons?
A **season** is a period of the year with a particular type of weather. Different parts of the world have different seasons. Countries far from the equator have four seasons: **spring, summer, autumn (fall), and winter**. Countries near the equator, like the Philippines, have **two seasons**: the **wet season** and the **dry season**.

## Why the Earth has seasons
It is not because the Earth is closer or farther from the Sun. It is because the Earth is **tilted**.

The Earth spins on an imaginary line called its **axis**. This axis is tilted at about **23.5 degrees**. As the Earth travels around the Sun, different parts of the planet tilt toward or away from the Sun.

- When your part of the Earth tilts **toward** the Sun, you get **summer** — the Sun's rays hit more directly, so it is hotter and days are longer
- When your part tilts **away**, you get **winter** — the Sun's rays come in at an angle, so it is cooler and days are shorter

The equator is always warm because it faces the Sun directly all year. The poles are always cold for the same reason — they never face the Sun directly.

## The four seasons (in temperate countries)

### Spring
Weather warms up after winter. Plants grow new leaves. Flowers bloom. Baby animals are born. Days get longer.

### Summer
The hottest season. Days are long and sunny. Plants grow fast. Many people go on vacation.

### Autumn (Fall)
Weather cools. Leaves change color and fall from trees. Farmers harvest crops. Days get shorter.

### Winter
The coldest season. Trees are bare. Some animals hibernate. In very cold places, snow falls. Days are short and nights are long.

## The two seasons in the Philippines
Because the Philippines is near the equator, it does not have four seasons. Instead:

### Wet season (roughly June to November)
- Heavy rain and typhoons
- Warm and humid
- Rice fields fill with water
- Farmers plant rice

### Dry season (roughly December to May)
- Little rain
- Sunny and hot
- Good for beaches and outdoor activities
- Farmers harvest crops

## How seasons affect daily life
- **Farming** — different crops grow in different seasons
- **Clothing** — we wear lighter clothes in summer and warmer clothes in winter
- **Activities** — swimming in summer, skiing in winter
- **Animals** — some migrate, some hibernate, some grow thicker fur
- **Plants** — some bloom in spring, lose leaves in autumn, and rest in winter

## Try this at home
Use a globe (or a ball) and a lamp. The lamp is the Sun. Tilt the globe slightly and walk it around the lamp. Notice how different parts of the globe face the lamp at different times. That's how seasons work.
