# Habitats
Where plants and animals live.

## What is a habitat?
A **habitat** is the natural home of a plant or animal. It provides everything the living thing needs: food, water, shelter, and space. Different animals live in different habitats because they need different things.

## Types of habitats

### Terrestrial habitats (on land)
- **Forests** — tall trees, many animals like monkeys, birds, deer
- **Grasslands** — open fields, home to grazing animals
- **Deserts** — dry and hot, home to camels and cacti
- **Gardens** — small habitats near homes, full of insects and birds
- **Rice fields** — wet and muddy, home to frogs, snails, and dragonflies

### Aquatic habitats (in water)
- **Freshwater** — rivers, lakes, and ponds. Home to fish, frogs, and water plants
- **Saltwater** — oceans and seas. Home to whales, corals, and seaweeds
- **Mangrove swamps** — where freshwater meets saltwater. Home to crabs, fish, and mangrove trees

### Aerial habitats (in the air)
- **The sky** — where birds and bats fly and hunt
- **Treetops** — the upper branches of a forest where many birds and monkeys live

## Philippine habitats
The Philippines has amazing habitats because it's a group of tropical islands:
- **Rice fields** — the country's most common habitat
- **Mangrove swamps** — vital nurseries for baby fish and crabs
- **Coral reefs** — underwater cities full of colorful fish
- **Rainforests** — home to tarsiers, hornbills, and thousands of unique species
- **Seashores** — where you'll find hermit crabs, seashells, and shorebirds

## Why animals live where they do
Each animal is suited to its habitat:
- **Fish** have gills to breathe underwater
- **Birds** have wings to fly and hollow bones to stay light
- **Camels** store fat in their humps to survive deserts
- **Frogs** have moist skin and can live both in water and on land

If you move an animal to a habitat it's not suited for, it won't survive.

## How habitats change
Habitats can be damaged or destroyed by:
- **Deforestation** — cutting down forests for farms or buildings
- **Pollution** — trash and chemicals in water and air
- **Overfishing** — taking too many fish from the sea
- **Climate change** — rising temperatures and changing rainfall

When habitats are destroyed, the animals and plants that live there lose their homes.

## Protecting habitats
- Plant trees and native plants
- Don't litter — trash can choke animals or poison water
- Support protected areas like national parks
- Learn about local wildlife and how to help them
