# Body Systems
How your body's different systems work together.

## Your body is a team
Your body is made of many **systems** — groups of organs that work together to do a job. Each system has its own role, but they all depend on each other. Together, they keep you alive, growing, and active.

## The main body systems

### Skeletal system
Your **skeleton** is made of **bones**. An adult has 206 bones.
- **Supports** your body
- **Protects** important organs (skull protects brain, ribs protect heart and lungs)
- **Works with muscles** to help you move
- **Makes blood cells** inside the bone marrow
- **Stores minerals** like calcium

### Muscular system
Your **muscles** pull on bones to make you move.
- **Voluntary muscles** — you control them (arm, leg, face)
- **Involuntary muscles** — work without thinking (heart, stomach)

Muscles work in pairs. When one contracts, the other relaxes.

### Circulatory system
Your **heart**, **blood**, and **blood vessels** work together.
- The heart pumps blood
- Blood carries oxygen and nutrients to every cell
- Blood carries waste away
- Blood vessels are the roads the blood travels on

### Respiratory system
Your **nose**, **windpipe**, and **lungs** help you breathe.
- You breathe in **oxygen**
- Oxygen goes to the lungs, then into the blood
- You breathe out **carbon dioxide** — a waste gas

### Digestive system
Your **mouth**, **esophagus**, **stomach**, and **intestines** break down food.
- Food is chewed in the mouth
- It travels down the esophagus to the stomach
- The stomach mixes it with acids
- The small intestine absorbs nutrients
- The large intestine absorbs water
- Waste leaves the body

### Nervous system
Your **brain**, **spinal cord**, and **nerves** control everything.
- The brain is the control center
- Nerves carry messages
- The spinal cord connects the brain to the rest of the body

## How they work together
Imagine you're running:
1. **Nervous system** sends signals to your muscles
2. **Muscular and skeletal systems** move your legs
3. **Circulatory system** pumps blood faster to deliver oxygen
4. **Respiratory system** breathes faster to get more oxygen
5. **Digestive system** supplies energy from food
6. All systems work as a team

If one system stops working well, others are affected.

## Keeping your systems healthy
- **Exercise** strengthens heart, muscles, and bones
- **Eat well** — fruits, vegetables, proteins, grains
- **Drink water** — keeps blood flowing and cells working
- **Sleep** — your body repairs itself
- **Wash hands** — prevents germs from causing illness
