# Parts of a Plant
How roots, stems, leaves, and flowers work together.

## A plant is a team
A plant is made of several parts, and each part has a job. Together, they help the plant get food, water, and air — and make new plants.

## Roots
Roots grow underground. They have two main jobs:
- **Hold the plant in place** so it doesn't fall over
- **Absorb water and nutrients** from the soil

Some plants have thick roots that store food, like carrots and radishes. Others have long roots that reach deep for water.

## Stems
The stem is like the plant's highway. It:
- **Holds the plant up** so leaves can reach the sunlight
- **Carries water** from the roots up to the leaves
- **Carries food** made in the leaves down to the rest of the plant

Some stems are soft and green, like in a tomato plant. Others are hard and woody, like in a mango tree.

## Leaves
Leaves are the plant's food factories. Inside each leaf, the plant uses sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make its own food — a process called **photosynthesis**. Leaves come in many shapes and sizes. Some are broad and flat, others are thin and needle-like.

## Flowers
Flowers are the plant's way of making seeds. They attract insects and birds with bright colors and sweet smells. When these animals visit, they carry pollen from one flower to another. This is called **pollination**, and it's how new seeds begin.

## Fruits and seeds
After pollination, many plants grow **fruits**. A fruit protects the seeds inside. When the fruit is eaten by an animal, the seeds may be dropped far away and grow into new plants. A **seed** contains a tiny baby plant and enough food to help it start growing.

## Try this at home
Carefully pull up a small weed from a garden. Look at its parts: roots, stem, leaves. Can you see how each part is different? Draw what you see and label each part.
