# Plant Reproduction
How plants make new plants.

## Two ways plants reproduce
Plants can make new plants in two main ways:
- **By seeds** — the most common way
- **By plant parts** — cuttings, tubers, and more

## Flowers and pollination
Most plants use **flowers** to make seeds. Inside a flower are two important parts:
- **Stamen** — the male part that makes **pollen**
- **Pistil** — the female part that receives pollen

For a seed to form, pollen must travel from the stamen of one flower to the pistil of another. This is called **pollination**.

How does pollen travel?

- **Insects** — bees, butterflies, and beetles carry pollen on their bodies as they visit flowers
- **Birds** — hummingbirds and sunbirds also pollinate flowers
- **Wind** — some plants, like grasses, release pollen into the air
- **Water** — some water plants release pollen into the water

## From flower to seed
After pollination, a **seed** begins to form inside the flower. The flower's petals fall off, and the base of the flower grows into a **fruit**. The fruit protects the seeds while they develop.

## Seed dispersal
When seeds are ready, they need to travel away from the parent plant. This is called **seed dispersal**. If all seeds fell right under the parent, they'd fight for water, sunlight, and space. Dispersal gives new plants a chance.

Seeds travel by:
- **Wind** — dandelion seeds float on the breeze
- **Water** — coconuts float across oceans
- **Animals** — eaten with fruit and dropped elsewhere
- **Bursting** — some pods explode, flinging seeds out
- **Sticking** — burrs stick to animal fur

## Seed germination
When a seed lands in good soil with water and warmth, it starts to grow. This is called **germination**. The seed sprouts a small root going down and a small shoot going up. Soon it becomes a young plant.

## Asexual reproduction
Some plants can make new plants **without seeds**. This is called **asexual reproduction** or **propagation**:

- **Cuttings** — cut a stem and plant it; it grows roots
- **Tubers** — potatoes grow new plants from "eyes"
- **Bulbs** — onions and garlic grow from bulbs
- **Layering** — a branch bends to the ground and roots form
- **Grafting** — joining a branch from one plant onto another

Farmers use these methods to grow plants that are exactly like the parent — good for fruit trees and garden plants.

## Try this at home
Cut the leafy top off a carrot and place it in a shallow dish of water. Leave it on a sunny windowsill. After a week, you should see new green shoots growing. That's asexual reproduction!
