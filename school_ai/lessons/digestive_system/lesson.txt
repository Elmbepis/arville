# The Digestive System
How your body turns food into energy and nutrients.

## What the digestive system does
The **digestive system** takes the food you eat and breaks it down into tiny pieces your body can use. It turns a sandwich into nutrients that feed your cells — and into waste that leaves your body.

The whole journey takes about **24 to 72 hours** from mouth to exit.

## The journey of food

### 1. Mouth
Digestion starts the moment you take a bite.
- **Teeth** chop and grind food into smaller pieces
- **Saliva** (spit) softens food and starts breaking down starches into sugar
- The **tongue** pushes food to the back of your mouth so you can swallow

### 2. Esophagus
The **esophagus** is a muscular tube that connects your mouth to your stomach. Muscles squeeze in waves to push food down — a process called **peristalsis**. This works even if you are upside down.

### 3. Stomach
The stomach is a muscular bag that holds food for a few hours.
- It churns food like a washing machine
- It mixes food with **stomach acid** and enzymes
- The acid kills germs and helps break food into a thick liquid called **chyme**

### 4. Small intestine
The **small intestine** is where most digestion finishes and nutrients are absorbed.
- It's about 6 meters long, coiled up in your belly
- Tiny finger-like bumps called **villi** line the walls to grab nutrients
- Nutrients pass from the food into your blood

### 5. Large intestine
The **large intestine** absorbs water and minerals from what is left.
- What remains becomes solid waste
- Helpful bacteria live here and help break down fibers

### 6. Rectum and anus
Waste is stored in the **rectum** and then leaves the body through the **anus**.

## Helpers along the way
Some organs help digestion without food passing through them:
- **Liver** — makes bile to break down fats
- **Gallbladder** — stores bile
- **Pancreas** — makes enzymes that break down food

## Keeping your digestive system healthy
- **Eat slowly and chew well**
- **Eat fiber** — fruits, vegetables, whole grains
- **Drink water** — helps food move through
- **Limit junk food** — too much sugar and fat is hard on the system
- **Exercise** — keeps muscles moving, including digestive muscles

## Try this at home
Take a bite of bread and chew it 20 times before swallowing. Notice how it tastes sweeter as you chew. That's saliva breaking down the starch into sugar!
