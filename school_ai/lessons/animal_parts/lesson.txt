# Animal Body Parts
How different animals use their bodies to move, eat, and survive.

## Every body part has a job
Animals come in many shapes and sizes, but every body part has a purpose. Legs help animals move. Mouths help them eat. Eyes help them see danger. Wings help them fly. When you look at an animal, you can often tell what it does by looking at its body.

## Moving: legs, wings, fins

### Legs
- **Four legs** — dogs, cats, horses, deer. Good for running on land.
- **Two legs** — birds, humans. Free the front limbs for other uses like holding or flying.
- **Six legs** — insects. Small and quick.
- **Eight legs** — spiders. Used for walking and catching prey.
- **Many legs** — centipedes and millipedes. Can have dozens or hundreds of legs.

### Wings
- **Birds** have feathered wings for flying. Some, like ostriches and penguins, have wings but cannot fly.
- **Bats** have leathery wings made of skin stretched over long finger bones.
- **Insects** have thin wings, often two or four.

### Fins
Fish have fins for swimming. A **tail fin** pushes them forward. **Side fins** help them steer and stop. **Dorsal fins** on top help them stay upright.

## Eating: mouths and beaks

Different mouth shapes suit different foods:

- **Sharp, hooked beaks** — eagles and hawks, for tearing meat
- **Long, thin beaks** — hummingbirds, for sipping nectar
- **Flat, wide beaks** — ducks, for scooping up plants and small animals in water
- **Strong, cracking beaks** — parrots, for cracking nuts and seeds
- **Sharp teeth** — lions and sharks, for tearing meat
- **Flat teeth** — cows and horses, for grinding plants
- **Long, sticky tongues** — anteaters and frogs, for catching insects

## Sensing: eyes, ears, noses

- **Eyes** — predators like eagles have eyes facing forward to judge distance. Prey like rabbits have eyes on the sides of their heads to see in all directions.
- **Ears** — bats and dolphins hear very high sounds that humans cannot hear. Elephants hear very low rumbles from far away.
- **Noses** — dogs have powerful noses that can smell things humans cannot. Moles use their noses to find worms underground.

## Protection: shells, quills, scales

- **Shells** — turtles have hard shells that protect them from predators.
- **Quills** — porcupines and hedgehogs have sharp spines. Predators that try to bite them get a mouthful of pain.
- **Scales** — fish and reptiles have scales that protect their skin.
- **Slime** — slugs and eels produce slippery slime to escape from predators.

## Body coverings

| Covering | Which animals | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Fur | Mammals | Warmth, camouflage |
| Feathers | Birds | Warmth, flight, display |
| Scales | Fish, reptiles | Protection |
| Skin | Amphibians | Breathing, moisture |
| Exoskeleton | Insects, crabs | Protection, support |
